# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Fine-Tuning Studio community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what benefits the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include sexualized language or imagery, trolling,
insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private
harassment, publishing another person's private information without permission, and
other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They
may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, and other
contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. They will communicate moderation
decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when someone is officially representing the
project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to a repository
maintainer through GitHub. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. Maintainers
will review reports promptly and fairly and will respect the reporter's privacy and
security.

Responses may range from a private correction, to a warning, temporary restriction, or
permanent ban, according to the severity and pattern of behavior.
